What it does

Three things, done properly

Import from anywhere

Drop in a link, a screenshot, or a TikTok, and Pomegranate pulls out just the ingredients and steps — nothing else.

Create shopping lists

Drag recipes onto shopping list that groups everything by aisle.

Get inspired

Tell Pomegranate what's in your fridge or just how you're feeling, and it'll suggest something from your saved recipes worth cooking tonight.

The name

Why Pomegranate?

A splash in every dish

Even a spoonful of arils adds a burst of color and tart-sweet flavor to almost anything you're cooking — the same way one good recipe can lift a whole week of meals.

Jewels, not just seeds

Cut one open and you'll find hundreds of glistening arils packed together. Every recipe you save here is a little jewel too — worth keeping, worth coming back to.

A French word, on purpose

Pomegranate comes from the French pomme-grenate — "seeded apple." France's culinary reputation speaks for itself.
