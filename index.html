Cook from what you save, not what you scroll.

Pomegranate pulls recipes out of any link, photo, or video, tidies them into one place, and turns your week into a shopping list — no scrolling past someone's life story to find the ingredients.

Launching soon on iOS and Android
What it does

Three things, done properly

Import from anywhere

Drop in a link, a screenshot, or a TikTok, and Pomegranate pulls out just the ingredients and steps — nothing else.

Plan the week

Drag recipes onto your days, and Pomegranate builds a grocery list that groups everything by aisle.

Make it healthier

Ask for a lighter version of any recipe and swap ingredients in a tap — with the original always one tap back.

Cooking better starts with saving better.
